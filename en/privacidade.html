Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

Blank Studio ("we") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we use it, and what rights you have, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

Blank Studio is a Portuguese digital agency specialising in web development, SEO, Google Ads and digital marketing. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at blankstudio.geral@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data you provide directly, through the contact and quote request forms on the site:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (when provided)
  • Company name and project details (when provided)
  • The content of the message you send us

We do not collect sensitive data or payment information through the site.

3. How we use your data

We use the data you provide exclusively to:

  • Respond to your contact or quote requests
  • Prepare proposals and communicate about potential projects
  • Deliver the contracted services, should you move forward with Blank Studio

We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and we never sell or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Sharing data

Your data is not shared with third parties, except where required by law or a competent authority. We may rely on third-party tools for the site to work (for example, hosting or font providers), which may process limited technical data — see our Cookie Policy for details.

5. Data retention

We keep the data we collect only for as long as necessary to respond to your request or, should you become a client, for the duration of our business relationship and for the period required by applicable legal and tax obligations in Portugal.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to the processing of your data
  • Request the portability of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at blankstudio.geral@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD).

7. Cookies

Our site uses cookies to ensure it works properly. To find out more about the cookies we use and how to manage them, see our Cookie Policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We recommend checking it periodically. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.

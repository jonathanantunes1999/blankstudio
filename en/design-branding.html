FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to create a visual identity?

Usually 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the complexity of the project and the number of revisions involved.

What's included with the logo?

The main logo, variations (horizontal, vertical, icon) and versions for light and dark backgrounds, in every format you need.

Do I receive brand guidelines?

Yes, we always deliver a brand guide covering colours, typography, spacing and logo usage rules.

I already have a logo — can you just create the graphic materials?

Yes, we also work with existing brands, keeping the visual coherence already in place.

How much does a branding project cost?

It varies with scope — from a simple logo to a full rebrand. Request a free, personalised quote.

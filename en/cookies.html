Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies the Blank Studio website (blankstudios.pt) uses, and how you can manage them.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly, remember your preferences or, in some cases, gather information about how the site is used.

2. What cookies we use

At present, our site only uses essential, technical storage:

  • Cookie preference — stores your choice ("Accept" or "Decline") from the cookie banner, so we don't ask again on every visit.

We do not currently use advertising cookies or traffic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics). If this changes in the future, this page will be updated and the consent banner will reflect that change.

3. Third-party cookies

The site loads fonts via Google Fonts and images hosted on external services. These providers may set their own technical cookies, outside our direct control, as part of loading those resources.

4. How to manage cookies

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies directly from the banner shown when you visit the site. In addition, most browsers let you:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close the browser

Note that blocking essential cookies may affect how some parts of the site work.

5. More information

To learn more about how we handle your personal data, see our Privacy Policy. For any question about cookies, contact us at blankstudio.geral@gmail.com.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy whenever necessary, particularly if we introduce new tools on the site. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
